## Design rule (Jill, Oct 23): the Biodome stays CLOSED
It is a sealed system to balance. Nothing opens it to the planet's atmosphere. Players can control how much sunlight gets in (more or less sun).
Things in the current game to check against this rule:
- "Scoop out extra algae" says the algae is dried OUTSIDE the dome. Change it so the algae stays inside (for example it goes onto the compost pile, so the matter is recycled, not removed).
- "Dome cover On/Off" is really a sun shade. Rename it (for example "Sun shade: more sun / less sun") and maybe make it a 3-step light control.
- Adding spare crates during the seasons brings in new matter from the ship's storage. Decide: allowed (through an airlock, only what was packed) or build-phase only?
- "Collect water from the glass" and "Turn the compost" already keep everything inside (recycling), so they fit the rule.
