# What the Keystone Biology exam tests (STEELS-aligned), and what the game may teach

Checked against PDE sources on Nov 5-8, 2026. The game uses ONLY these standards. The 2014 Keystone
Assessment Anchors and Eligible Content (BIO.A.x.x.x / BIO.B.x.x.x) are no longer used anywhere in the game.

## Module 2: Continuity and Unity of Life: Biodiversity, Genetics, and Changes over Time

- Natural Selection and Evolution: S, T, U, W, X

## Assessment boundaries (what is NOT tested), from the STEELS Foundation Boxes
- A: "Assessment does not include the identification of specific cell or tissue types, whole body systems, specific protein structures and functions, or the biochemistry of protein synthesis."
- B: "Assessment does not include interactions and functions at the molecular or chemical reaction levels." (Emphasis: functions at the organism system level, such as nutrient uptake, water delivery, and organism movement in response to neural stimuli.)
- C: "Assessment does not include the cellular processes involved in the feedback mechanism." (Examples: heart rate response to exercise, stomate response to moisture ...)
- D: "Assessment does not include specific gene control mechanisms or rote memorization of the steps of mitosis."
- E: "Assessment does not include specific biochemical steps." (Emphasis: inputs and outputs of matter and transfer/transformation of energy in photosynthesis.)
- F: "Assessment does not include the details of specific chemical reactions or identification of macromolecules."
- G: "Assessment should not include identification of the steps or specific processes involved in cellular respiration." (Emphasis: conceptual inputs and outputs.)
- H: "Assessment is limited to proportional reasoning to describe the cycling of matter and flow of energy."
- I: "Assessment does not include deriving mathematical equations to make comparisons."
- J: "Assessment does not include the specific chemical processes of either aerobic or anaerobic respiration."
- K: "Assessment does not include the specific chemical steps of photosynthesis and respiration."
- L: "Assessment is limited to provided data."
- M, N, O, S, V, W, X: no boundary (N/A).
- P and Q: "Assessment does not include the phases of meiosis or the biochemical mechanism of specific steps in the process."
- R: "Assessment does not include Hardy-Weinberg calculations."
- T: "Assessment does not include other mechanisms of evolution, such as genetic drift, gene flow through migration, and co-evolution."
- U: "Assessment is limited to basic statistical and graphical analysis. Assessment does not include allele frequency calculations."

## Teacher decisions (Nov 2026)
- Course order (v76, Nov 11, 2026): six worlds that match the teacher's six course units. Units 3 and 4 both belong to
  "Interdependent Relationships in Ecosystems": Unit 3 Ecological Relations = I, L, M (Module 1); Unit 4 Ecosystem
  Disrupters = N, O, V (Module 2).
- Levels whose whole topic was outside STEELS are REBUILT around an in-scope standard (same place, biome and platforming).
- Supporting vocabulary that is needed to talk about a standard (cell/tissue/organ, population/community/ecosystem,
  producer/consumer, gene/allele, chromosome) is kept.
- Protein synthesis: DNA sequence -> mRNA -> amino acid order -> protein shape -> job. No codon-chart decoding, no tRNA/anticodons, no ER -> Golgi path.
- Middle school refreshers (added v65, Nov 10, 2026): cell parts (nucleus, ribosome, cell membrane, mitochondrion,
  chloroplast) are middle-school STEELS 3.1.6-8.B ("the ways that parts of cells contribute to the function"), and the
  four kinds of large carbon-based molecules support 3.1.9-12.F ("amino acids and/or other large carbon-based molecules").
  Keystone items assume this background, so short review content is back in levels 1-3, 2-1, 2-2 and 2-3. Every review
  item says "Refresher", carries the tag "review" (so it never appears in mixed gates or boss fights), and has topic
  review-cell-parts, review-macromolecules, review-chloroplast or review-mitochondria. Codes: nucleus/ribosome items A,
  other cell parts B, macromolecules F, chloroplast E, mitochondria G.
- Exploration areas (Willow Creek, coves, falls, Biodome): cards stay as exploring flavor; only cards that clearly match a STEELS standard carry a tag.

## Out of scope (removed from the platformer question banks, except the tagged middle-school refreshers above)
Characteristics of life; prokaryote/eukaryote and detailed organelle identification (ER, Golgi, lysosome, vacuole...); membrane structure and transport (diffusion,
osmosis, active transport, endo/exocytosis); properties of water; macromolecule details beyond the refresher, dehydration
synthesis/hydrolysis; enzymes; ATP-ADP cycle details; chloroplast/mitochondrion structure; water cycle and nitrogen cycle;
types of symbiosis (mutualism, commensalism, parasitism as named categories); biome identification; names/order of the
phases of mitosis and meiosis; DNA technology (genetic engineering, cloning, gel electrophoresis, gene therapy);
genetic drift, founder effect, bottleneck, gene flow; Hardy-Weinberg and allele-frequency math; nature-of-science
vocabulary (observation/inference/hypothesis/theory/law).
