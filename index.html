About Earthling Field Guide

Earthling Field Guide: A Keystone Biology Mission
Created by J.E. Lynch

A free learning game for Pennsylvania high school biology. Players explore as an alien field researcher, discover vocabulary and key ideas, and practice the Keystone Biology eligible content.

Standards

  • Pennsylvania Department of Education. Keystone Exams: Biology. Assessment Anchors and Eligible Content. Harrisburg, PA.
  • Pennsylvania Department of Education. Pennsylvania Integrated Standards for Science, Environment, Ecology, Technology and Engineering (STEELS), Grades 9–12. Harrisburg, PA.

About the content

  • All questions, notes, and activities were written for this game. They are aligned to the standards above but are not official Keystone Exam items.
  • The data sets in "Read the Data" (for example, potato cores in sugar solutions and enzyme activity) are illustrative, modeled on classic classroom labs. They are not real measurements.
